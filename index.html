our approach

Every visit starts at the mirror, not the chair. You show us the photos you saved. We tell you what your hair or skin can really do, what it will cost, and how often you will need to come back. Some days the honest answer is a trim and a good conditioner.

  • ten minutes, first

    Every service opens with a consultation at the mirror. It is never billed and never rushed.

  • price before you sit

    Each service lists its cost and its time. If your hair needs more of either, we tell you before we start.

  • booked at real length

    Silk presses, locs, braids and natural curls get the hours they actually take, with stylists who work on them daily.
